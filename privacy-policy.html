Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Lexidence handles your data. Lexidence is a personal dictionary app designed to help you save, organize, and practice words and short phrases.

Developer

Lexidence is provided by Vasyl PD. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer using the contact information provided in the app or on its Google Play listing.

Data stored locally

Lexidence stores your dictionary content locally on your device. This includes saved words and phrases, translations, notes, learning statuses, progress information, app settings, and related data needed for the app to work.

Dictionary content is not uploaded

Lexidence does not upload your saved dictionary, notes, or learning progress to a Lexidence server. The app does not provide user accounts, cloud synchronization, or online storage for your dictionary. Some optional device services and third-party SDKs process information as explained in the sections below.

Automatic translation

When you select two specific, supported languages and use automatic translation, Lexidence may download the required Google ML Kit translation models. These downloads use internet data and device storage. Text entered in the word and translation fields is translated on the device, rather than sent to a Lexidence server or an online translation service. Automatic translation is unavailable when “Other” is selected or the selected languages are not supported by ML Kit.

Firebase Analytics

Lexidence uses Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and improve the experience. Firebase Analytics may collect technical and usage information, including app interactions, app version, device information, analytics identifiers, and approximate location inferred from an IP address.

No dictionary content in analytics

Lexidence does not intentionally send your saved words, translations, or personal notes to Firebase Analytics.

Language identification

Lexidence uses Google ML Kit Language Identification to identify text languages for pronunciation. The input text is processed on the device and is not sent to Google servers for language identification. ML Kit may send technical performance and usage metrics to Google, including diagnostic information about how its APIs are used, but not the input text or the resulting output.

Voice input and microphone

When you tap a microphone button, Lexidence opens Android’s system speech recognition interface. The speech recognition provider handles microphone access and audio according to its own privacy policy and your device settings; it may process speech online. Lexidence receives the recognized text but does not record, save, or send microphone audio to its own servers. Voice input availability and language support depend on your device and its installed speech recognition services.

Pronunciation and text-to-speech

When you play a pronunciation, Lexidence passes the relevant word or phrase to the Android text-to-speech engine selected on your device. Depending on the installed provider and your device settings, the engine may process this text locally or online under its own privacy policy. Lexidence does not record the generated audio or send it to a Lexidence server.

CSV import and export

Lexidence lets you export your dictionary to a CSV file and import compatible CSV files. These actions happen only when you choose them. An exported file may include saved words, translations, notes, and learning information, and is saved outside the app. During import, Lexidence accesses the file you select, validates its entries, and saves accepted entries locally. Existing words are not duplicated during import.

Exported file responsibility

After you export a CSV file, you control where it is stored and whether it is shared. Keep exported files secure and share them only with people, apps, or services you trust.

No account required

You do not need to create an account to use Lexidence. The app does not require your name, email address, phone number, or password for its dictionary and learning features.

Advertising

Lexidence uses Google AdMob. When access to word suggestions is restricted by learning progress, you may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement to unlock suggestions. This is optional; a reward is provided only when the advertising service confirms that it was earned. When your dictionary contains at least 100 words, Lexidence may also display a banner advertisement in Training mode.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as the Android advertising identifier (AD_ID), other device identifiers, IP address, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, secure, personalize where permitted, and measure advertising. Lexidence uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent choices where required by applicable law and Google policies.

Reminders and update notifications

If you enable learning reminders and allow notifications on your device, Lexidence may schedule a local reminder at least 12 hours after the app leaves the foreground, subject to its daytime scheduling and Android system settings. The app uses counts of new and learning words from its local database to prepare the reminder. It does not upload your dictionary entries or these word counts to schedule reminders. The learning reminder switch in the app controls learning reminders only.

Lexidence uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications about new app versions. Google may process a Firebase installation identifier, a messaging token, device information, IP address, and delivery diagnostics to route and troubleshoot these notifications. Update notifications do not contain your saved dictionary content. You can control notification permissions and channels in Android settings.

Third-party services

Lexidence uses services provided by Google, including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google ML Kit, Google Play services, Google AdMob, and the User Messaging Platform. For voice input and pronunciation, it also uses the speech recognition and text-to-speech providers available on your Android device. These services and providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

For additional information, see the Google Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information, and ML Kit Terms & Privacy.

Permissions and file access

On supported Android versions, Lexidence requests notification permission for learning reminders and app update notices. For voice input, Lexidence launches Android’s system speech recognition interface rather than accessing the microphone directly; the system recognition provider manages any necessary recording permissions. Before deleting all dictionary words, Android may request biometric or screen-lock authentication. Lexidence receives only the authentication result, not your biometric data, PIN, password, or unlock pattern. For CSV import and export, you select or create files through the Android file picker, and Lexidence accesses only the selected files. Advertising services may use network access and the device advertising identifier as described above.

Data deletion

You can delete individual words or all words within Lexidence. You can also clear the app’s local data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Exported CSV files are stored outside the app and must be deleted separately. Clearing local app data does not necessarily delete information already processed by third-party service providers; their own privacy and retention policies apply.

Children

Lexidence is a general learning tool and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Third-party services may process technical information as described in this policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Lexidence evolves. If its data handling practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures as appropriate. The “Last updated” date above indicates when this policy was most recently revised.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy or data handling in Lexidence, please use the contact option in the app or the developer contact details available on Google Play.
